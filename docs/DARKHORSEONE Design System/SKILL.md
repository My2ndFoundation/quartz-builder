## Key Brand Elements

**DARKHORSEONE LIMITED** — "makes your business an unexpected winner"

**Color Palette:**
- Green Series: #0e4a3a (main), #0a3a2d (deep), #072a20 (ink)
- Cream Series: #f4efe3 (main bg), #ebe4d2 (cards)
- Text Colors: #102018 (ink), #3d4d44 (ink-soft), #6f7b73 (muted)
- Accent: #d9a441 (gold), #8a2e1b (danger red)
- Warm, professional aesthetic with cream backgrounds

**Typography:**
- **Gaoel**: Logo "DARKHORSEONE" only
- **Syncopate**: All headings, hero/display text (ALL CAPS, wide letter-spacing)
- **Inter**: Body text, descriptions
- **JetBrains Mono**: Labels, metadata (ALL CAPS, wide letter-spacing)

**Visual Identity:**
- Checkered flag logo symbol (ascending diagonal, represents winning)
- Warm cream backgrounds with deep green accents
- Bold, technical typography with wide letter-spacing
- Paper texture overlays for warmth
- Checker pattern decorative elements
- Eyebrow labels

**Design Patterns:**
- Cards with colored top borders (3px)
- Section headings with Syncopate (ALL CAPS, 700 weight)
- Eyebrow labels in mono caps
- Warm, approachable professional aesthetic

**Tone:**
- Professional yet warm and approachable
- Confident and action-oriented
- Client-focused (second-person: "your business")
- Technical precision with human warmth
- No emoji usage
